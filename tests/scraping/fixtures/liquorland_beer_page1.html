How can I place myself on a self-exclusion list?

Liquorland is dedicated to the safety and wellbeing of our customers and aims to promote the responsible consumption of alcohol. By excluding yourself from Liquorland services you will no longer be able to place orders on the Liquorland, First Choice Liquor Market, Vintage Cellars and Coles Online websites.​

To voluntarily exclude yourself please email the following details to: liquorlandordersupport@liquorland.com.au

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number

If you or someone you know are struggling with the consumption of alcohol you can find support services at DrinkWise
